```
Editorial macro photograph of an empty dark forest-green felt surface with a single faint circular indentation visible in the center, where an object once rested but has been removed. Cool overhead light grazing the texture so the indentation is barely visible — only suggested by a small shadow. Museum-grade material texture, shallow depth of field, fine grain. Pure black surrounding void on the edges. Mood: the trace of what was removed, presence as absence. Style: editorial still life photography, restrained palette. Avoid: text, people, faces, modern objects, neon, oversaturation.
```
