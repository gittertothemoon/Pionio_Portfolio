# Morning Image · 9 LUG

Foto editoriale con overlay parola "trascrizione" (la parola va nella caption, non sull'immagine).

## Prompt AI (ChatGPT / Nano Banana / DALL-E)

```
Editorial macro photograph of a pair of dark vintage over-ear headphones resting on their side on a dark walnut wooden surface, the ear cups facing the camera, the cable coiled loosely beside them. Cool side light from camera-left at 4000 Kelvin grazing the leather cushions and metal headband, deep forest-green (#306b4d) shadow tone on the wood. Museum-grade material detail, shallow depth of field on the closest ear cup, fine soft grain. Pure black surrounding void on the edges. Mood: listening as the act that precedes writing, the silent tool of the transcription. Style: editorial product photography, restrained palette, contemplative stillness.

Negative: text, people, faces, hands, modern brands, neon, oversaturation, microphones, notebooks, paper.
```

## Pipeline

1. Genera 3-4 varianti
2. Salva PNG in `assets/morning-raw.png`
3. `cd src && node render.cjs`
