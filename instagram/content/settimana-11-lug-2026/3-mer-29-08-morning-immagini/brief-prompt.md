# Morning Image · 29 LUG · IMMAGINI

Foto editoriale per la parola "immagini" — la materialità e il peso degli archivi visivi.

## Prompt AI (ChatGPT/DALL-E/Nano Banana)

```
Editorial macro photograph of a large rectangular photographic glass plate resting on a dark slate or stone surface, the glass catching cool side light from camera-left and revealing a faded ghost-image of an old landscape etched into the emulsion. Subtle deep forest-green tint in the reflected light on the stone underneath. Dust motes catching the light edge. Shallow depth of field, museum-grade detail, fine grain. Mood: weight, history, the materiality of images. Pure black surrounding void at the edges. Style: editorial documentary photography, restrained palette. Avoid: text, screens, people, faces, modern devices, neon, oversaturation.
```

## Pipeline
1. Genera 3-4 varianti
2. Salva PNG in `assets/morning-raw.png`
3. `cd src && node render.cjs`
