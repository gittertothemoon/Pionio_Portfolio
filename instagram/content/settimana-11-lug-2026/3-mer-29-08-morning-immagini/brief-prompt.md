Editorial macro photograph of a heavy stack of glossy photographic prints lying on a dark concrete surface, the topmost print partially curling at the corner under its own weight. Cool side light from camera-left grazing the surface. Subtle texture, shadows from the stack's mass. Deep forest-green accent reflection on the concrete (subtle). Mood: weight, accumulation, materiality of images. Shallow depth of field, museum-grade detail. Style: editorial documentary photography, restrained palette, pure black surrounding void.
